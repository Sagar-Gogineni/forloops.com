What does your AI system do?

HIGH RISK

Your AI system likely falls under Annex III of the EU AI Act.

  • What this means: Full compliance required before deploying in the EU. Risk management, bias audits, technical documentation, human oversight, and EU database registration.
  • Deadline: August 2, 2026 (or December 2027 if the Digital Omnibus delay passes). Either way, compliance work takes months.
  • Penalty for non-compliance: Up to 35,000,000 EUR or 7% of global turnover, whichever is higher.

LIMITED RISK

Your AI system has transparency obligations under Article 50.

  • What this means: Users must be informed they're interacting with AI. AI-generated content must be machine-readable labeled.
  • Deadline: August 2, 2026.
  • Good news: Compliance is straightforward. Transparency disclosures and content labeling can be implemented in a day.

MINIMAL RISK

No mandatory requirements under the EU AI Act.

  • What this means: Your AI system doesn't trigger mandatory compliance obligations. Voluntary codes of conduct are encouraged.
  • However: If your system evolves (new features, new data types, new use cases), your classification could change. Internal tools that start making decisions about people can shift to high-risk.
  • Recommended: Turn on audit logging anyway. It's free and gives you a documented compliance trail.
